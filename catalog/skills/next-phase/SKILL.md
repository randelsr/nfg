You are to proceed with the next phase of the implementation. First you are to review the completion document from the preceeding phase implementation, eg phase_n_completed.md (if applicable). Then review this phase's implementation document, eg phase_n_description.md. Once you are complete this phase, create a completion document highlighting all findings and items that you were unable to complete in the completion document, eg ./.plans/phase_n_completed.md.
